# Questions and answers

Short answers to the questions owners ask first.

## Is it only for barbecue?

No. A new Smokebox starts empty. You add your own dishes, rename the stations and choose whether you cook ahead. Only the demo is a barbecue kitchen.

## Does it need the internet?

No. Smokebox is one file and works without internet.

## Can two tablets share one board?

Not yet. Each device has its own board. Two tabs in the same browser on the same device stay in step.

## Does it charge cards?

No. It only writes down how an order was paid. Keep your card reader.

## Where is my data?

In this browser on this device. Save a backup every day at close.

## What does 86 mean?

Sold out or off the menu. The **86 board** lists what is out and why.

## What is a starter estimate?

A number that came with the app, such as a prep time or a cost. It is a guess until you replace it with your own.

## Does Smokebox know how long cold food may keep?

No. For cold food in a fridge you type the days it may stay and the hours it may be out. Smokebox counts against those numbers. Use the limits your local health department gives you.

## Why is there no Cabinet tab?

The Cabinet shows only when you tell Smokebox you cook food ahead. Tap **Settings**, then **Dishes and prices**, and tap **Yes, I cook ahead**.

## Can I try it without risking my data?

Yes. See Watch the demo.

## Can I change a price on an open order?

New tickets use the new price. Tickets already on the board keep theirs.

## Has it been used in a real kitchen?

It has been tested in a browser on a computer, not in a working kitchen. Try it on your own tablet before a busy service, and keep your usual system beside it until you trust it.
