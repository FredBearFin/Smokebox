# Taking an order

Start an order, add dishes and notes, send it to the kitchen, and add to it later.

## Start the order

Tap **New ticket**. Tap a dish to add it. Tap it again to add one more. A dish marked 86 (sold out) cannot be added.

## Customer and order type

Under **New or open order**, keep **New order**. Under **Customer**, type a name in the **Customer name** box or tap a quick name. Leave it blank for Walk-up. Choose how the order came in: Dine-in, Takeout, Drive-thru or Delivery app. Each type has its own time limit before a ticket turns amber and then red.

## Allergies and notes

Under Any food allergy?, tap **Allergy**, then tap what the customer is allergic to. Put allergies first in the note. Cooks read the first line first.

## Rush

Switch on **Rush ticket** when a customer is waiting. Every dish starts at once and the ticket goes to the front of the board.

## Send it

Tap **Send to kitchen**. Each station sees only its own dishes. Pay now, or leave it on Pay later and take payment from **Orders** when the customer is ready.

## Add to an order that is still open

Tap **New ticket** again and choose **Add to open order**. The new ticket is added to the same order.

## Fixing a mistake

- **Undo** appears for 10 seconds after an action. After that, fix it by hand.
- To cancel a ticket, open its small menu on the board, tap **Void ticket** and say why. A cancelled ticket is left out of your sales and money, and shows only in the count of voided tickets.
