# How you serve

Counter or table, kitchen tickets or none, your own order buttons and your own currency symbol.

## Open it

Tap **Settings**, then **How you serve**. Every change saves at once, and a bar offers **Undo**. Under your dishes you may also see the question "How do you serve?". Its answers only set the options below.

## Counter service

The switch says On or Off beside its name. Turn on **Counter service** if you hand food over at the counter. The board shows one list of orders, with no station tabs, no Start step and no late colors. When an order is paid, its tickets finish and clear themselves. With **Pay later**, the ticket waits until you pay from **Orders** or tap **Bump**. Leave it off if cooks work from tickets.

## Order buttons

These are the quick buttons on the order screen, like Walk-up, Pickup and tables. The ones in use show as buttons: tap one to remove it. Type a name and tap **Add button** to add one (up to 12), or tap **Use the starting list** to start again. With none, the order screen shows only the name box.

## Currency symbol

Pick $, € or £, or **Your own**: one to three characters, like kr. It shows with every price, and open screens update when you close the sheet. It does not convert anything.

## An order you did not finish

If the page closes or reloads in the middle of an order, tap **New ticket**. Your dishes come back with the note "Restored your unfinished order". Tap **Clear order** to start fresh.
