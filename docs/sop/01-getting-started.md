# Getting started

Add dishes, send a test order, take payment and save a backup. About ten minutes.

## 1. Add your menu

1. A new Smokebox starts empty on purpose. Tap **Add my menu** on the welcome screen. To see a busy kitchen first, tap **Watch the demo**.
2. Type what the dish is called and its price. As you type, a line shows how Smokebox reads it, like "Reads as $12.50". Tap where it is made. The starting stations are Kitchen, Prep, Fryer and Cold and drinks. Rename or drop any (see Setup recipes).
3. Tap **Save and add another** until your menu is in. Then tap **Save and finish**.

Change dishes and prices later: tap **Settings** (the three-line button at the top right of the board), then **Dishes and prices**. A dish you remove goes to **Removed dishes**. Tap **Bring it back** to put it back.

The arrow buttons beside each dish change the order they appear in. The menu section list on a dish's row moves it to another menu section.

Have your menu in a file? See Import or export your menu.

## 2. Set your tax and hours

Tap **Settings**, then **Tax, hours and PIN**. Enter your sales tax (0 if you charge none) and your opening hours. A PIN is optional. It keeps the back office for managers only.

Dishes and prices asks if you cook food ahead. See Cooked-ahead food and portions.

## 3. Send a test order

1. Tap **New ticket**.
2. Tap a dish, then tap **Send to kitchen**.
3. The ticket appears on the board. Tap **Start**, then **Done** on the dish, then **Bump** the ticket.

A test order counts in your reports. To remove it, cancel it (see Taking an order).

## 4. Take payment

Tap **Orders**. For cash, tap the Pay button on the order. It shows the total, like Pay $8.50 cash. For a card, a tip or a discount, tap **Tip or discount**, choose how it was paid, then tap **Mark paid**.

Smokebox only writes down how the order was paid. It never charges a card.

## 5. Save a backup

Tap **Back office**, then **Menu, prices and shop**. Find Data, backup and reset and tap **Download backup (JSON)**. This saves a file. Keep a copy off this device: email it to yourself or put it on a USB drive.
