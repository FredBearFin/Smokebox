# Close the month

Move old, finished history out of this device so it stays quick. You save everything to files first. Nothing is removed until step 3.

The screen is called **Close the month**. If a PIN is set, the PIN pad opens first.

1. **What this does**. Under **Close everything before**, the date starts on the first of this month. Smokebox shows what moves out (days, orders, tickets and activity records) and what stays: the last 35 days, today, every open order and open ticket, your menu and settings, the end-of-day totals, and all food-safety records. If you pick a date inside the last 35 days, it tells you where it stops instead. Tap **Next: save the files**.
2. **Save the month files now**. Tap **Save the month files now**. Your browser downloads five files: a backup of the whole kitchen, three spreadsheets (orders, tickets and days) and a list of what is in them. Look in your downloads folder and check each one is there. If the browser asks to allow several downloads, say yes. Select **I saved the files and I can find them**, then tap **Next: confirm**.
3. Type CLOSE. Type the word CLOSE, in any case. Then the red **Close the month** button works.

After step 3 the history leaves this device. For 7 days you can still undo it here; after that, the files you saved in step 2 are the only copy. The last screen says "Done. The month is closed." and has **Undo**, which says "Undone. Your kitchen is back as it was, with all the history." Tap **Done** to leave.

To bring everything back later: Back office, Data, Restore, and pick the backup file.

## When it refuses

Each refusal ends with "Nothing was removed."

- Something changed since you saved the files (a new day started or a record changed): save the files again in step 2, then close. A page reload does the same.
- On a device that works through the main computer, it cannot be closed there. Do it on the main computer's own screen or the main tablet.
- In the demo there is no real history. Tap **Back to my kitchen** first.
- With nothing old enough to close, the screen says "There is nothing to close yet." and shows no next step.
- If the browser would not save the change, nothing was removed.
