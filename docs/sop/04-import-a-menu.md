# Import or export your menu

Bring dishes in from a spreadsheet or a point-of-sale export, instead of typing each one.

## Open it

Tap **Settings**, **Dishes and prices**, **More ways to add dishes**, then **Have a menu file? Import it**. Tap **Choose a menu file** or paste a list, then tap **Preview**. Nothing is added yet.

## Read the preview

Smokebox says what kind of file it thinks this is, such as a spreadsheet or a Lightspeed items export. Square, Toast and Clover exports work the same way. Each row shows its dish, its price and "Read as $14.00", the price it understood. Fix a name or price in the row, or clear the box to leave a dish out. A row with a problem says what is wrong and starts cleared.

- Prices may be in cents. If every price is a large whole number, Smokebox asks. Tap **These prices are in cents**, or **These prices are in dollars** to undo it.
- Columns it does not use are listed, like "Ignored column: Calories". Notes and descriptions are not kept. Only the name, price, station and section are.
- Pick one station for every dish in **Put all these dishes at**, and one section in **Put all these dishes in**.
- New sections in the file? Tap **Add these sections**.

## Add them

Tap **Import** with the number shown, like Import 12 dishes. Changed your mind? Tap **Remove these** and the whole import comes off the menu.

Adding dishes by hand in Dishes and prices works just as well.

## Export your menu

Tap **Export my menu** on the same screen. It saves your dishes as a spreadsheet file, smokebox-menu.csv, that you can edit and import again.
